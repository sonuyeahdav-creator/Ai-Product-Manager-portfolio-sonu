Product Requirements Document: Autonomous Viral Reel Engine for D2C Brands

An automated system that converts any raw e-commerce product page into high-converting, motion-graphics video ads in under 35 seconds. Replaces manual agency workflows, slashing cost-per-creative from $1,200 to $0.05.

1. Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
  • • Creative Fatigue: E-commerce brands on Meta & TikTok suffer from ad burnout every 7–10 days. Producing 15–20 high-quality reels/month costs $15k–$25k.
  • • Agency Latency: Traditional motion graphic designers take 4–7 days per video iteration, making fast trend exploitation impossible.
  • • The Opportunity: Deliver studio-grade Ali Abdaal / Hormozi kinetic motion graphics fully deterministically in under 60 seconds with zero manual video editing software.
2. User Personas & Target Segments
  • • D2C Brand Founders & Performance Marketers: Need 50+ ad variants for weekly A/B testing across cold audience acquisition.
  • • Marketplace Sellers (Flipkart, Amazon, Shopify): Have high-performing SKUs but zero video production talent or budget.
  • • Social Media Agencies: Need to scale video output 10x without hiring 10 more After Effects animators.
3. Core Functional Requirements (FRs)
  • • FR-1 (Multimodal Ingestion): Parse e-commerce URL, extract title, price, discount, review count, and high-res product cutout images.
  • • FR-2 (Viral Hook Synthesizer): Score and generate 5-scene narrative scripts based on proven psychological retention archetypes.
  • • FR-3 (Neural TTS Engine): Generate human-cadence voiceovers with customizable speech rate, pauses, and pitch sync.
  • • FR-4 (Headless Frame Rendering): Render 1080x1920 60 FPS motion graphics with hardware acceleration using GSAP and headless Chromium.

End-to-End AI Engineering Pipeline

Visualizing how data flows from a raw e-commerce URL to a broadcast-grade 1080x1920 MP4 file ready for Meta & TikTok ad managers.

01

Multimodal Ingestion

Scrapes DOM, pricing, discount, rating, and extracts high-resolution transparent product cutouts.

Input: Product URL
Output: Product Meta JSON
02

Hook & Script LLM

Synthesizes 5-scene viral narrative using Ali Abdaal / Hormozi retention taxonomy and word counts.

Model: Gemini 1.5 Pro / GPT-4o
Output: Timestamped Script
03

Neural Voice & Audio

Synthesizes energetic voiceover, transcribes word timestamps with Whisper, and layers ambient SFX.

Engine: Edge TTS + Whisper
Output: Master Audio MP3
04

Headless Motion Render

GSAP springs render deterministically in headless Chromium via HyperFrames and muxes with FFmpeg.

Engine: HyperFrames + FFmpeg
Output: 1080x1920 MP4
