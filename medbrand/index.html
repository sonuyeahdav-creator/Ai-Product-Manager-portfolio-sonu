Common Questions

Everything you want to know before you register.

I am not fluent in English. Can I still join?

Yes. The first weeks are built to raise your professional English from intermediate to working level. We assess your level in the orientation and split training accordingly.

Do I need healthcare experience?

No. You need discipline and a working smartphone or laptop. AI-driven clinic workflows and HIPAA-conscious communication are trained from zero.

Is the salary figure realistic for Madagascar?

That is the range remote clinics commonly pay for qualified SDR and operations staff. Your final rate depends on your English level, skills, and the employer.

Are these remote jobs?

Yes. MedBrand connects you directly to independent global clinics hiring remote staff. You work from home with international teams.

What happens after I register?

You receive the curriculum, join the private WhatsApp group, and get an invite to the live orientation session where we cover the program and confirm your seat.
